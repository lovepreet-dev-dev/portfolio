Loan Manager — offline finance back-office

A fully offline, zero-install loan management system shipped to a small finance business and used every day. Pure JavaScript on Node's built-in SQLite engine, so there are no native modules to compile and the whole thing ships as a portable Windows bundle with an embedded runtime.

  • Four interest models — reducing-balance EMI, flat, fixed-installment and interest-free — across daily, weekly, fortnightly, monthly and custom schedules
  • Correct by construction — balances are recomputed from immutable payment history rather than mutated in place, covered by a 35-assertion test suite over the money math
  • Automatic allocation across penalty, interest and principal, with printable receipts, statements and agreements
  • Ships itself — NSIS installer and portable ZIP built by a GitHub Actions pipeline, plus crash-safe staged backup/restore

Agentic AI · Research Flagship

AgriShift-Sentinel — agentic plant disease diagnosis

An agentic layer wrapped around a 38-class PlantVillage ResNet-18 ensemble. A vision agent, a confidence/quality agent and an orchestrator implement three-tier routing: answer directly, retrieve and reason, or escalate to a human expert. A conformal prototype “Shift Sentinel” runs before the healthy shortcut, because a low-confidence “healthy” prediction can be a missed disease.

  • 75.8% → 14.2% harmful-action rate vs the always-trust-the-model baseline
  • AUROC 0.981 separating held-out in-domain from web out-of-domain images, at 0% in-domain false positives
  • Grounded answers — bge-small + FAISS retrieval over an agronomy corpus, so treatment advice cites sources instead of hallucinating
  • Image-quality gating (Laplacian blur + brightness) and 3-model ensemble disagreement catch confident misclassifications thresholding alone missed

Research Autonomous swarm

CASSIA — calibration-aware sequential swarm

A backbone-agnostic inference layer for image classification under domain shift that never defers to a human. Instead it buys additional evidence: deterministic counterfactual view agents that change one nuisance factor at a time while preserving the putative disease.

  • 24.2% → 33.2% top-1 on out-of-domain field images, at ~3.2 views per image instead of all ten
  • Bayesian competence-and-dependency fusion learns each agent's class-conditional confusion and discounts agents whose errors correlate
  • Budgeted orchestration purchases the next view by exact expected information gain per unit cost, stopping on an autonomous stability quorum
  • Calibrated by source-only shift rehearsal — no test label ever tunes the system

Research Federated

FedCEA — client discordance under drift

A federated pilot investigating which layer carries the most client disagreement under different non-IID skew regimes, and whether correcting that layer helps generalisation to unseen domains.

  • The most discordant layer moves with the skew regime — the first feature block dominates under feature skew, the classifier head under label skew
  • Correcting it did not reliably improve unseen-domain accuracy — a clean negative result suggesting feature diversity can be a feature, not a defect
  • Benchmarked against FedAvg, SCAFFOLD and FedPVR across feature, label and mixed drift
  • Runs unattended on the NITJ H100 cluster through single-MIG PBS jobs

Federated learning for plant disease detection

A 5-client FedProx simulation on PlantVillage with ResNet-50 — 50 communication rounds, 10 local epochs each, mixed-precision training, logging per-round accuracy, precision, recall and F1.

  • Benchmarked FedAvg, FedProx, FedOpt, FedMA and SCAFFOLD under non-IID client splits to quantify what privacy costs in accuracy and convergence
  • Authored a review paper on privacy-preserving federated learning for precision agriculture, covering communication efficiency and client heterogeneity
