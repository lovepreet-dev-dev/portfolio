Remindly Privacy Policy

Effective 22 October 2026 · applies to the Remindly Android app (app.remindly.reminders)

The short version

Remindly does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. Everything you create stays on your device.

What the app stores, and where

Your reminders, lists, notes, completion history and settings are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. Remindly has no account system, no servers, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no network access. We, the developer, never see any of it.

Backups

If Android backup is turned on for your device, the system may include Remindly's data in your device backup (for example to your Google account). That backup is created and stored by Google under Google's Privacy Policy; Remindly never sends data anywhere itself.

Permissions

NotificationsTo show your reminders when they are due.
Alarms & reminders (exact alarms)So reminders ring at the exact minute you chose.
Full-screen notificationsTo show a due reminder over the lock screen, like an alarm. Optional.
Run at startupTo re-arm your reminders after the phone restarts.
Vibrate / keep awakeSo a reminder can vibrate and appear while the phone is idle.

Deleting your data

You can delete any reminder or list in the app. Uninstalling Remindly, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes all of its data from your device.

Children

Remindly is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
