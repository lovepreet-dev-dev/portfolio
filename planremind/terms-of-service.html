1. What the app does

PlanRemind builds study schedules. You tell it what you are preparing for, how long you have, and when you are free. It takes the syllabus for that exam from a set bundled inside the app, arranges those topics into a dated schedule on your device, and reminds you when each session starts.

No account is required. Version 1.0.0 works entirely offline: it makes no network requests, and your study plan and progress are stored only on your device.
