PlanRemind

Native Android app · Kotlin · Jetpack Compose · version 1.0.0 · released by Lovepreet Sharma

Kotlin Jetpack Compose Room WorkManager Offline-first

PlanRemind turns an exam, a timeline, your subject strengths and the hours you actually have free into a complete day-by-day study schedule — then reminds you when each session starts. Twenty-two built-in exam syllabi can be planned entirely without AI, and Room keeps every plan, session and progress update available offline.

Highlights

  • 22 exams, no AI required — curated syllabi make plan creation fast, predictable and usable without sending study details anywhere.
  • Gap-free plans from 7 to 365 days — deterministic scheduling distributes topics across your real availability.
  • Reminder-safe — a rolling 14-day scheduling window avoids Android alarm limits while keeping upcoming sessions covered.
  • Fully offline in 1.0 — no account, no tracking, no analytics. Your plan never leaves your device.
