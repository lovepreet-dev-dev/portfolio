Privacy Policy for PlanRemind

Effective date: August 26, 2026 Last updated: August 26, 2026 Application: PlanRemind (Android package com.aistudio.studyplanner.qzkwpy) Version this policy describes: 1.0.0 Developer: Lovepreet Sharma Contact: joshibidowali123@gmail.com Postal address: Bidowali, PO Channu, District Shri Mukatsar Sahib, Punjab 152101, India

1. Summary

PlanRemind builds day-by-day study schedules for exam preparation.

PlanRemind does not collect any data. Version 1.0.0 has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party trackers. It does not send anything to us or to anyone else, because it does not make network requests at all.

Everything the app knows about you — your exam, your study hours, your plan, your progress, your streak — is created on your device and stays there.

2. What we collect

Nothing.

We operate no server that receives data from this app. We cannot see your study plan, your progress, your device, your location or your identity, because none of it is ever transmitted.

3. What the app stores on your device

All of the following is stored in the app's private storage area, readable only by PlanRemind and by you:

WhatWhyWhere
Exam name, plan length and study time windowsTo build and rebuild your scheduleLocal database
Your generated study sessions, with dates, times and subjectsTo show your plan and set remindersLocal database
Completion, skip and missed status per sessionTo track progress and your streakLocal database
Your per-subject confidence levelsTo weight the scheduleLocal settings store
App preferences such as theme and notification settingsTo remember how you like the appLocal settings store

Uninstalling PlanRemind deletes all of it. There is nothing left behind on any server, because nothing was ever sent to one.

4. Built-in exam syllabi

PlanRemind ships with syllabi for 22 exams bundled inside the app. Choosing an exam reads a file that is already on your device. No lookup, request or search leaves your phone, which is why plans generate instantly and work with no internet connection.

5. Google backup

Android's own backup service is enabled for this app. If Back up to Google Drive is switched on in your device settings, Android may copy PlanRemind's local database and preferences into your personal Google account, so your plan survives a phone reset or transfer.

That backup is performed by Android, held in your own Google account, and governed by Google's privacy policy. We have no access to it. You can disable it in your device settings under Google > Backup, or exclude this app there.

6. Permissions and why the app asks

The app never asks for your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, files or phone identity, and it cannot read them.

7. The unused internet permission

Version 1.0.0 declares the internet permission but makes no network requests. The networking library is still compiled into the app from an earlier design in which unlisted exams were sent to an AI service to have a syllabus generated. That feature is switched off in this release and cannot run.

We are telling you this because you can inspect the permission list yourself and we would rather explain it than have it look like something is hidden. If a future version turns that feature back on, we will update this policy and the Play Data safety declaration before shipping it, and the store listing will say so.

8. Children

PlanRemind is a study tool intended for exam candidates, and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone of any age, because we do not collect information at all. There is no chat, no user generated content, no social feature and no way for users to contact each other.

9. Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export or delete on request. You remain in full control:

  • See your data: it is all visible inside the app.
  • Delete your data: uninstall the app, or use Android Settings > Apps >

PlanRemind > Storage > Clear data.

  • Delete your backup: manage it in your Google account backup settings.

If you are in a jurisdiction with statutory data rights, such as the GDPR or the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection Act, those rights apply against whoever holds your data. For this app that is you and, for backups, Google.

10. Security

Your study data never leaves your device in transit, so there is no transmission to intercept. On the device it is held in app-private storage, which Android prevents other apps from reading. Data on a modern Android device is encrypted at rest by the operating system.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change what the app does with information, we will update this policy and change the "Last updated" date above before the new version is released. Material changes — in particular, any version that begins sending data anywhere — will be described in the release notes on the Play Store listing.
